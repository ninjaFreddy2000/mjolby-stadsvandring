10th January 1987. Sodom, Lund Outside temperature: -24 Inside temperature: 4,000 With ice crystals in my hair, I went into the club that was packed with people, loud music, and cigarette smoke. On the dancefloor I met a girl and we danced to a booming Gloria Gaynor song, while talking at extremely high volumes. An hour after midnight, we exchanged telephone numbers. That was the night I met my wife. /Per Björk The Sodom nightclub which once occupied this building, opened it s doors around 1969, offering a combination of dancing and whisky. Over the years, the venue has housed a number of different clubs (Sargasso, Blue Ice, Number 15) and was also a video rental shop.
