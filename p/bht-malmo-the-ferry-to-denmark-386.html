Ferries left from here to Copenhagen a place that offered richer entertainment and greater anonymity than Malmö. It was also in Denmark that the Circle of 1948 and R dstrumperne women's movement were formed. In 1971, the Danes arranged the first women's camp on Fem . Women from around the world participated, and it fostered networks of Nordic activists that would go on to play major roles in the growing lesbian movement. In 1975, the first lesbian week was organized on Fem with 130 participants. Foto: Malmö Stadsarkiv Text adapted from: A Ride to Pride en kartguide till Malmös hbtqi-historia, Malmö Stadsarkiv
