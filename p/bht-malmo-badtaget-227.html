"Badtåget"

The Heleneholmsstigen cycle path follows the former railway line between Malmö and Trelleborg that opened in 1886.

The Heleneholmsstigen cycle path follows the former railway line between Malmö and Trelleborg that opened in 1886. A branch line was added in 1904, linking Vellinge to Skanör and Falsterbo. Officially known as the Falsterbo line, it soon picked up the nickname Badtåget (bathing train), because it served so many swimmers heading to the coast. On Sundays during the summer season, an additional steam train puffed all the way to beaches at Näset. The Södervärn-Falsterbo section was closed down on 22 August 1971. "Heleneholmsstigen" is also the name of a short animated film by the filmmaker Henrik Möller, set right here. Check it out on YouTube!
