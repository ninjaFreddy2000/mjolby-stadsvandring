Hotel Statt - A Proper City Hotel

Even though Hässleholm was still waiting for the decision on whether it would actually be granted city status, officials felt an urgent need for an elegant City Hotel .

Even though Hässleholm was still waiting for the decision on whether it would actually be granted city status, officials felt an urgent need for an elegant City Hotel . So urgent in fact, that they started building the hotel despite a rather worrying radio silence from the national authority responsible for the decision... The reason for this radio silence was that the government's initial response to Hässleholm's initial request included a list of financial commitments that would go along with becoming a city. Suffice to say, it was not cheap. Hässleholm's officials refused the proposed terms, and responded with a counter offer. Months passed with no word from Stockholm. When almost a year had passed, everybody started getting rather nervous - perhaps starting to build the city hotel was too much of a gamble after all... On the 19th of December, a short message was received from Stockholm - Hässleholm's application was approved! And so it was, that Hotel Statt opened its doors on January 1st, 1914 - the same day that Hässleholm's inhabitants woke up in their beds for the first time as proper city folk.
