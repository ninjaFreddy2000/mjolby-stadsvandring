Self-guided city walks in Mjölby, Sweden

Stadsvandring.io is a free web app (PWA) for self-guided walking tours in Mjölby, Ydre, Kinda, Mullsjö, Värnamo, Motala, Valdemarsvik and the surrounding Östergötland countryside. Explore 84 sights, buildings, people and historic events one place at a time — with an interactive map, walking routes, collectible stamps and quizzes.

No installation needed: Stadsvandring.io runs straight in your browser on phone and desktop, and you can switch between English and Swedish at any time inside the app. Below is an English overview of every place. The full place articles are written in Swedish — open the app and tap the flag to read them in English.

Mjölby · 43 places

MjölbyLocality · An old mill village on the Svartån that became a railway hub and the regional centre of south-west Östergötland.
SvartånWaterway · The river whose rapids gave Mjölby its mills — and its very reason to exist.
Mjölby kyrkaChurch · The town church on the village's highest point, with a medieval tower that survived the great fire of 1771.
Mjölby järnvägsstationStation · The station that turned a mill village into a railway junction and regional hub.
Mjölby Stadshotell (gamla Järnvägshotellet)Hotel · A timber hotel opposite the station, built just after the railway arrived in 1873.
Stora TorgetSquare · The village's historic heart, by the old crossroads toward Småland.
Mjölby hembygdsgård (Norrgårdsholmen)Museum & heritage · Falu-red museum houses on an island in the river, with a fine marquetry collection.
Galleria KvarnenTrade & commerce · The town's shopping gallery, its name a nod to the milling heritage.
Gamla Stadshuset / Stadshuset i MjölbyBuilding · The town hall by the gallery and library; a Carl Milles sculpture stands at the entrance.
KvarnparkenCafé & restaurant · A riverside park and venue with a restaurant, pub and the statue of Skånska Lasse.
Konditori HörnetCafé & restaurant · A cosy, central classic patisserie next to the library.

Ols Möbler (idag Mio Mjölby)Industry & business · A furniture dynasty founded in 1887 and still run by the same family.

SkänningeLocality · One of Sweden's oldest towns and a medieval power centre, today part of Mjölby municipality.

Vårfrukyrkan ('Garpekyrkan'), SkänningeChurch · The German merchants' mighty brick church — one of few large medieval brick churches in Östergötland.
S:ta Ingrids klosterruinMonastery ruin · Sweden's first convent for women (Dominican).
S:t Olofs kloster & Petrus de DaciaMonastery ruin · The Dominican friary in Skänninge where 'Sweden's first author' worked.
Adolf Fredrik LindbladPerson · Composer born in Skänninge, 'the Swedish Schubert', teacher of Jenny Lind.
Lindbladsparken & bysten av A.F. LindbladArt & statue · A park in Skänninge with a bust of composer Adolf Fredrik Lindblad.
Ture Lång (Rolandsstaty), SkänningeArt & statue · A steel giant on Skänninge's square, a symbol of justice in the medieval German tradition.
Svaneholms borgruinCastle ruin · The ruins of a 14th-century castle on a headland in Lake Kilarpesjön.
Öjebro stenvalvsbroBridge · An old stone-arch bridge in the former mill village of Öjebro.
Stadsbranden 1771Event · The fire that almost wiped out the mill village — and lets us know its medieval layout today.
Dackefejden i Mjölby (1542–43)Event · Mjölby was the northernmost point reached by the Småland rebels of the Dacke War.
Mjölby AI FF / VifolkavallenSport · The town's football club, with its home ground at Vifolkavallen.
Fem blev en — Mjölby kommun (1971)Event · In 1971 the towns of Mjölby and Skänninge and three rural municipalities were merged into today’s Mjölby Municipality.
